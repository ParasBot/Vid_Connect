# 📹 VidConnect

**VidConnect** is a social discovery platform that enables users to connect with like-minded individuals through short video introductions. By recording a video and sharing interests, users are matched with potential friends, collaborators, or communities with similar vibes.

---

## 🌟 Features

- 🎥 **Video Profiles**: Record and upload short video introductions to showcase your personality.
- 🤝 **Smart Matching**: Match with others based on shared interests, goals, and personality traits.
- 🌐 **Community Discovery**: Find and join interest-based communities.
- 🔒 **Privacy First**: Choose who sees your video and manage your visibility settings.
- 📱 **Responsive Design**: Works seamlessly on both web and mobile devices.
